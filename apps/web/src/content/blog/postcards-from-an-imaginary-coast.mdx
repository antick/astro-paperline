_These postcards describe an imaginary coast. They are sample stories, not travel recommendations._

## Postcard one: the harbor

Dear friend,

The harbor is smaller than the map made it seem. Two boats would almost fill it, and this morning there are exactly two. One has a blue sail. The other appears to have been painted the color of yesterday's sky.

I found a table facing the water and ordered something warm. The postcard rack beside the door showed the same view in four seasons. I chose the empty card instead.

## Postcard two: the inland path

The path behind the harbor climbs through a stand of low trees. From the top, the sea looks like another sheet of paper laid beneath the hills. The houses are so small that a finger could hide the whole village.

## On the back of the card

- **Weather:** enough wind to turn a page.
- **Sound:** a rope tapping against a mast.
- **Something to remember:** the blue cup on the harbor table.

I will leave the rest unwritten. A postcard needs a little empty space for whatever the reader brings to it.

_Until the next imaginary stop._
